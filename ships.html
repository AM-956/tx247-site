The Coalition Shipyard

Supporter Commissions

Wars are won in the shipyard. Supporters commission Coalition staff to build large-grid hulls to spec — vanilla weapons, vanilla blocks, identical for every player — delivered in game with ownership transferred and a backup snapshot taken at handover. The dollar figures are support levels, and the hull is our thank-you. Classes run by PCU at the one honest rate: 10,000,000 SC = $1.
