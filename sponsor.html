Guardrails

Convenience & status.
Never pay-to-win.

Sponsor buys comfort and recognition — never power nobody else can earn. The stipend is tradeable currency, the discount is on packs anyone can buy, and the slot gets you into the same fair fight as everyone else.

No exclusive weapons or hulls

There is no Sponsor-only ship, gun, or block. Anything that flies or fights can be earned in game.

No protection money

Sponsor status never shields a grid from lawful PvP under the Rules of Engagement. The law applies by flag, not by wallet.

Cancel anytime

Subscriptions cancel in one click — no ticket, no guilt trip. Credits already delivered are yours to keep.
