How Buying Works

Checkout here.
Claim in game.

1 · Check out on the site

Pay here — card or Apple Pay via Stripe. Link your Discord at checkout so we know whose hangar it is.

2 · Claim in game

Credits and ships deliver to your account in game. Interim: while the webstore finishes, orders run through Discord tickets and staff fulfill by hand — same prices, same rate.

3 · Backed up at delivery

Every delivered ship is snapshotted at handover. Verified server-fault losses are restored free, with a ticket and proof.

Retired rails

Cash App / PayPal manual payments are retired. If anyone asks you to pay that way for TX247 goods, it's not us — open a ticket.
