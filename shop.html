How Support Works

Support the server.
Claim in game.

1 · Support right here

Pick a pack above and check out securely through Stripe — it takes a minute, and your receipt is your claim ticket. Commissions still start with a Discord ticket, so staff can scope the build with you.

2 · Claim in game

After checkout, open a ticket in the Discord #order-desk with your receipt. Staff link your account and deliver your credits and ships in game by hand, as a thank-you for your support — same levels, same rate.

3 · Backed up at delivery

Every delivered ship is snapshotted at handover. Verified server-fault losses are restored free, with a ticket and proof.

Retired rails

Cash App / PayPal manual payments are retired. If anyone asks you to pay that way for TX247 support, it's not us — open a ticket.
