The Director · Live Now

The Living Sky

TX247 doesn't run an empty sandbox with a chat box. Our server-side Director keeps all four factions on the air around you — patrols, convoys, pirate traffic — and it remembers your standing with each of them.

Start a fight over Earth, or call !911, and the GDC scrambles a quick-reaction wing to you. Most servers give you ships. Ours talks back.
