About TX247

TX247's Galactic Coalition Series provides a top quality Space Engineers dedicated server with a small, friendly community of gamers.

Established in 2013, TX247 strives to provide the best server experience possible as we continue to expand our player base and embark on a new adventure in the stars.

Three worlds — Earthlike, Pertam and Europa — four factions, one resource law: uranium and platinum live only on Europa, and asteroids are gravel. Mining happens on planets. War happens over freight.

Join us today and enjoy the journey!

The Director · Live Now

The Living Sky

TX247 doesn't run an empty sandbox with a chat box. Our server-side Director keeps all four factions on the air around you — GDC patrols working the lanes over Earth, DIG convoys calling their runs in the frontier, Federation traffic over Pertam, and the Wake, the MPE fleet channel, saying the quiet part out loud. Wherever you fly, the sky has traffic. The traffic has opinions.

And it answers. Announce yourself to a GDC patrol and you're logged and waved through — hail the Federation, talk to DIG Dispatch, test the pirates' sense of humor. Every exchange is kept: your standing with each faction moves on what you do and say, and the world treats you accordingly the next time you cross its sky.

It's wired into the stations, too. Docking runs through traffic control, not hope: hail Port Authority, get a real pad and a window, and the board holds it for you. Petram Control, DIG Dispatch and the Wake run their own docks their own way — and the Anchorage asks no questions, ever. It just remembers who didn't bring a gift.

Most servers give you ships. Ours talks back.
