🎯 For hunters

The board is the contract list. A name appears the moment a bounty posts — 5M SC, stacking to 25M — with their wanted stars and every escape on their record beside the price. Collect by bringing the pilot down lawfully: the bounty pays out through your MailBox, settleable with !tx247 claim. When a contract goes live on a name, the board marks it — the prize, the window, and how many hunters are already on it.

⚖️ If your name is up here

Your bounty is your fine, and it's yours to end: pay it with !tx247 payfine and your stars, your warrant and your name on this board clear on the spot. Run, and every escape is recorded next to the crime — the board does not forget how you ran. The only other way off is absence: fourteen straight days dark with the fine unpaid, and the Coalition writes the debt off. Your escape record stays.
