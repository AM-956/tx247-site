The Lore

The story so far

Every faction on TX247 has a history — written in our Discord RP series. This is the war you're enlisting in.

I

The Exile

Earth's rebels, exiled by the GDC to the hard rock of Pertam and left to disappear. Instead they built The Red Federation — and swore to fly home and take Earth back.

II

The Uranium Kings

Founded in 2199, the Denarian Industrial Group bought the wreckage of the 2201 ore crash for pennies — and woke up owning over half the galaxy's uranium. Every reactor in the sector burns DIG fuel.

III

The Satreus Front

The GDC came to build, allied with DIG — and collided with the TRF splinter on Satreus. The Coalition won, drove the Federation out, then did the unthinkable on Pertam: proposed a truce, and withdrew.

IV

The Pirate Shadow

Into the vacuum sailed the Minmatar Pirate Empire — mercenaries and outcasts under no truce at all. The empires signed their peace. The pirates never did.

V

The New Dawn — Season 1

The shipyards are lit, the hangars are open, and all four powers are recruiting. Season 1 begins with you. Faction loyalty is permanent — choose like it matters.

The Four Powers

Where each flag comes from

GDC — Galactic Defense Coalition

Earth's shield and the law of Protected Space: patrols, ports, and the Fighter Corps that answers when you call for help. The GDC exiled the rebels — then offered them peace, and keeps the fleet that makes it stick. "THE LINE HOLDS."

TRF — The Red Federation

Exiled from Earth and dumped on Pertam, the Federation did not die in the dust — it got organized. Miners, soldiers, and shipwrights who answer to no Earth admiral and bow to no pirate toll. "TOGETHER WE FIGHT, TOGETHER WE PROSPER."

DIG — Denarian Industrial Group

Founded 2199, the Group moves the ore that moves the galaxy: convoys, claims, refineries, and the best-paid contracts in TX247 space. DIG does not start wars — DIG ends shortages. "BE A MINER, BE A LEGEND, BE THE FUTURE."

MPE — Minmatar Pirate Empire

The toll every trader fears and the freedom every outlaw dreams of: take what you can hold, answer to no law, and fly under a flag that marks you a target in every sky, at all times. "NO RULES, NO MERCY, NO STOPPING."
