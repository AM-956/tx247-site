Getting in

On PC

Join Game → Direct Connect → enter 191.101.30.133:29016. The six server mods download automatically from mod.io when you join — nothing to install by hand.

On console (Xbox / PlayStation)

Join Game → server list → press the E (EOS) button next to the search bar to switch to the crossplay list, then find TX247 S1 or direct-connect the address above. Crossplay runs on Epic Online Services — the Steam server list won't show us.

Pick your faction

Join GDC, TRF, DIG or MPE at the in-game Faction screen — or stay independent. There is no changing sides. Full breakdown on the Factions page.

Survival is on — including food

Food & farming are ON. Plan your meals, grow or trade for food, and don't fly hungry. Slots are 10 for now — we grow as the community comes back.

The World & Resource Law

Three worlds, three jobs

GDC Protected Space

🟦 Earthlike

The breadbasket. Basic ores, the calmest sky on the server, protected by the GDC. New-player water — build your first base and your first ship here.

TRF + DIG Frontier

🟥 Pertam

The frontier. Gold, silver and magnesium wealth under red dust. Moderate danger, real money — the Federation's exile home and DIG's richest claims.

Contested Front

🟪 Europa

Ice, danger, and the two metals that matter most. Highest NPC traffic on the server — and the only place the endgame minerals spawn.

⚠️ Uranium & platinum: Europa only

URANIUM and PLATINUM spawn ONLY on Europa. Reactor fuel and jump-drive metal sit behind the front line on purpose. If you want the endgame, you go to Europa — or you buy from someone who did.

⚠️ Asteroids carry no ore

Asteroid stone refines to gravel only. Mining happens on planets: freight between worlds, set up outposts, or buy ingots from the factions — DIG sells the full mined catalog. Salvage from wrecks and derelicts is always legal. Meteor ore drops are off.

The Director · Live

The sky talks — and it keeps the record

The TX247 Director — the system we call the Living Sky — runs the four factions as a living world: radio traffic, answered hails, remembered standing, and traffic control at the stations. It runs entirely on the server — console players get the same living sky, and there is nothing to install.

📡 Faction radio — always on

All four factions broadcast in game chat: GDC patrol sweeps, DIG convoy calls, Federation traffic, the Wake saying the quiet part out loud. Listen before you fly — the traffic tells you what kind of sky you're entering.

📻 Hail & announce

Type !hail plus a faction — or !announce — in global chat (the tx247. commands work too). Announce yourself to a patrol and you're logged and waved through. Stay silent in protected space and you'll be remembered for that instead.

🧭 Standing is memory

Every exchange moves your standing with that faction — gains are capped, so it can't be farmed. Warm standing gets you waved through and cleared fast. Cold standing gets you challenged — and denied at the dock.

🛬 Docking runs through control

!dock at any hub and traffic control assigns you a real pad with a 10-minute window: Port Authority at the Port, Petram Control at the Redoubt, DIG Dispatch at the Exchange, the Wake at the Anchorage. !depart releases your pad early. The Anchorage asks no questions.

Grid Storage

Store a ship.
Free the PCU.

Quantum Hangar is gone. Use the vanilla Services Terminal: store a whole ship offsite — it stops counting against PCU — and pull it back out at any Services Terminal.

It's built into the game, so it's console-safe by design, and a stored grid keeps its ownership. Park the fleet, keep the sky clear.

FAQ

Asked on day one

Can console players really play here?

Yes — the server runs crossplay on EOS with a three-planet world (the console limit), data-only mods, and no client-side scripts. Join via the E (EOS) server list. See "Getting in" above.

Do I need to install the mods myself?

No. The six mods download automatically from mod.io the first time you join. Updates arrive the same way.

Why is there no uranium on Earth?

By law — TX247 law. Uranium and platinum spawn only on Europa so the endgame runs through the contested front: freight, escorts, trade and war all flow from that one rule. DIG sells the full mined catalog if you'd rather pay than fight.

How does food work?

Apex Survival food & farming are enabled. Grow food at your base, carry meals on long hauls, or trade — DIG crews make good grocers.

How do I buy a ship or credits?

On the Shop and Ships pages. Checkout happens here on the site; delivery lands in game. While the webstore finishes, orders run through Discord tickets and staff fulfill by hand.

What's coming next?

Already live: the TX247 Director's Living Sky — NPC radio chatter, hail & announce replies, standing memory, and docking control. Still in development, no dates promised: territory law enforcement, distress response, bounties, faction war events, MailBox Terminals for in-game claim of webstore orders, the MES Content Pack flying TX247 fleets, and the GDC Port of Authority hub station.
